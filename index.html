Round the Block — Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 2, 2026 · closed-beta legal version 2026-10-02.1

Local storage and developer access

Round the Block does not automatically upload your location, walking history, walk logs, or microphone audio to the developer. The app does not operate a Round the Block account service or backend that receives those records. There are no advertising or analytics integrations in this version of the app.

Walk logs are stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone. They may include precise locations, timestamps, selected routes, navigation progress, user actions, and instructions presented or spoken by the app. The app also stores appearance preferences, the accepted legal version and text identifier, acceptance time, app version, and update-check status locally.

Location and voice services

Precise location is used to find a nearby mapped street, plan and follow a block route, and provide retrace and return guidance. While navigation is active, location use continues if the screen is off or the app is in the background. Stopping navigation ends the app's active location updates.

Microphone access enables voice commands while the app is in the foreground with its display on. The app does not save microphone recordings to a walk log. Audio is delivered to your device's configured Android speech-recognition service, which may send audio to remote servers. Location, speech output, and update checks also use device or third-party services, including Google Play services, Google Play, and your selected speech engine. Those providers' processing is governed by their own policies and device settings; this notice does not promise that those providers retain no information. Declining microphone permission leaves touch controls available.

Nearby map downloads

When you start a walk, the app uses a trusted location to request nearby OpenStreetMap streets and paths from the public Overpass service at overpass-api.de over HTTPS. Requests identify a shared geographic area rather than your exact starting coordinate, but the area can still reveal your location. The provider also receives your IP address and an app identifier. Its handling and retention are governed by its own policies; the developer does not control or promise deletion of provider records. No walk log, walking history, or microphone audio is included in map requests.

Downloaded maps are cached privately on your phone, with up to five areas retained. Maps up to seven days old are reused without a map request. If a refresh fails, a saved map up to thirty days old may be used with a spoken warning. Android may clear cached maps, and clearing app storage or uninstalling removes them. A new area requires internet access; a walk already started uses its loaded map without further map requests. Missing, outdated, or incomplete mapping may prevent a route or produce inaccurate guidance.

Sharing a log voluntarily

The developer cannot access a locally stored walk log through a Round the Block server. If you choose to share a log, it may disclose precise locations and places you have visited. Sharing must be initiated by you, with a warning and a choice of destination. No log is uploaded automatically.

If you choose to send a log or support message to the developer, the developer and the service used to deliver your message may receive its contents and any associated contact information. Such a submission is an exception to the absence of automatic developer collection; it must not be described as “zero information retained” by every recipient.

Retention and deletion

Support messages and attached logs sent to support.roundtheblock@gmail.com are retained while the support request is being handled and deleted from the developer's support mailbox within 90 days after the request is resolved. You can request earlier deletion by emailing the same address. This is a manual support-mailbox policy, not automatic deletion by the app. Copies in your own email account, or retained by email providers under their own policies, are outside the app's deletion controls.

Local audit logs remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app; no automatic expiry is implemented. Tap the logo and choose Delete walk logs while navigation is stopped to remove stored logs and the app's temporary export copy. Copies you export or send are outside the app's deletion controls and must be removed separately from their destination.

Walk logs are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-transfer backups by this app's backup rules. Android may still back up appearance settings. Exporting or emailing a log may place a copy in a destination you select, including cloud storage. Other device services and backup tools are subject to their own behavior.

Updates and contact

Changes to app data handling will be reflected in an updated notice. Privacy questions and requests concerning information you voluntarily send to the developer may be directed to support.roundtheblock@gmail.com.
